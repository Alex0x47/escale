# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Escale contributors and maintainers commit to making participation welcoming,
respectful, and harassment-free, regardless of experience, identity,
background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Assume good intent while addressing the substance of disagreements.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials or private
  store data.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope and move disagreements to an
  appropriate private channel when requested.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to `pro@alexandre-grisey.fr`. Alexandre Grisey will
review reports privately and may edit or remove contributions, issue a warning,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant. Reports
will be handled with discretion, subject to legal obligations and the safety of
those involved.
